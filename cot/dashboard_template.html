Oil COT positioning monitor

Summary for the selected week

One card per contract, focused on Managed Money, the speculative funds. Pick any past week with the selector above to see how positioning stood then and how it changed from the previous week. All contracts here are 1,000-barrel contracts (or the equivalent), so 1,000 contracts = 1 million barrels. Every figure is explained in the guide at the bottom.

Positioning by trader group

Every trader group in every contract, with Managed Money highlighted. Net position = long minus short. Compare the selected week with the previous week, and with four weeks ago (roughly a month). The five groups add up to zero in each contract: every long has a short on the other side, so when funds are net long, other groups are net short in total.

How extreme is Managed Money positioning?

A percentile ranks this week's net position against the weekly readings of the past year (and three years). The bars below turn that rank into real numbers: the left end is the lowest net position in the window (0th percentile), the right end the highest (100th percentile).

Grey band = the middle 50% of weeks (25th to 75th percentile). Dark tick = median. Orange dot = the selected week. Percentiles only use data available up to each date, so past weeks are judged on what was known then.

History charts

The dotted vertical line marks the selected week. Use the Charts buttons at the top to change the time span of every chart at once.
